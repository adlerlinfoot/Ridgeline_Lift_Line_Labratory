Model rules, assumptions & interpretation

Decisions & memory

Choose the admissible lift minimizing valley travel + remembered queue wait + lift ride time. Equal minima use seeded random choice. Remote memories update only on physical visits. The lodge slider sets each skier’s chance of spawning at Main Lodge and walking to a lift; others start uniformly by distance along connecting base paths (a sole admissible lift uses its base). Opening queues are observed as zero simultaneously.

Skier classes

25% all-mountain, 35% vacationers, 15% lappers, 25% mixed. Lappers split 10:2:5 into park, L1 race, and assigned full-run specialists. Mixed skiers freeze up to two experienced lifts with the lowest personal mean waits at 40% of the time available from arrival to closing; a sole experienced lift remains sole.

Timing & closing

Instant boarding on capacity-paced service slots. Empty opportunities expire. No queue abandonment before closing. At 16:00 boarding ends; queues exit, riders and descending skiers finish safely. Arrival mode adds skiers until minute 210, then selects existing skiers to leave at the same Poisson rate. Selected skiers finish their committed lap; no random personal-stay departures. Fixed-mode skiers circulate until close.

Research limits

Geometry, speeds, capacities and population shares are synthetic. Queue balance is an accounting identity; these plots do not prove mathematical stability. Infrastructure comparisons combine capacity, terrain and access changes. Unfinished waits at closing are exported separately. The 3D skeleton uses supplied node elevations and interpolated route heights, wrapped onto a schematic conical body by default. It is schematic; the missing terrain surface is not reconstructed.
