Model rules, assumptions & interpretation

Decisions & memory

Choose the admissible lift minimizing valley travel + remembered queue wait + lift ride time. Equal minima use seeded random choice. Remote memories update only on physical visits. Opening queues are observed as zero simultaneously.

Skier classes

25% all-mountain, 35% vacationers, 15% lappers, 25% mixed. Lappers split 10:2:5 into park, L1 race, and assigned full-run specialists. Mixed skiers freeze up to two experienced lifts with the lowest personal mean waits at 40% of their planned stay; a sole experienced lift remains sole.

Timing & closing

Instant boarding on capacity-paced service slots. Empty opportunities expire. No queue abandonment before closing. At 16:00 boarding ends; queues exit, riders and descending skiers finish safely. Arrival-mode stays are uniform from 120–360 minutes. Fixed-mode skiers circulate until close.

Research limits

Geometry, speeds, capacities and population shares are synthetic. Queue balance is an accounting identity; these plots do not prove mathematical stability. Infrastructure comparisons combine capacity, terrain and access changes. Unfinished waits at closing are exported separately. The missing terrain heightfield is not reconstructed.
